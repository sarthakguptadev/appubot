The full list, with what each one was used for, is `packages/chat-core/src/credits.json` (shown in the apps under Settings › About › Credits). The licences of npm packages, fonts, icons and speech models are in the built app's THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt (`apps/desktop/scripts/notices.mjs`).
